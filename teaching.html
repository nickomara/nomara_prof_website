Public engagement

Science outreach & service

I regularly bring Earth and climate science to the public and to students of all ages — from elementary classrooms to museum floors — and contribute service to the scientific community.

  • Science Fridays visiting science educator (2020–2022) — annual guest lectures on paleoclimate for fifth-graders at PS 205, Brooklyn, via virtual tours of the Lamont-Doherty Core Repository.
  • American Museum of Natural History — Sun & Earth Day (2018–2019) — taught the public about glacial flow and the geological history of glaciers in New York City.
  • NYC Submerge (2018–2019) — showed children and parents how past climate is uncovered from ocean sediment.
  • Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory Open House (2017–2019) — designed and ran demonstrations on reconstructing Earth's past with sediment cores.
  • Upper Nyack Elementary Science Fair (2018) — taught grades 1–4 about plankton and paleoceanography.
  • Judging & review: AGU Outstanding Student Presentation Award judge (2022–present); Regeneron Westchester Science & Engineering Fair judge (2023–present); YIBS grant & poster reviewer (2022–present); reviewer for Nature Geoscience, Science Advances, Nature Communications, GRL, and many other journals.
